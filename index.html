    How to climb

    One mountain a day, the same for everyone. It changes at midnight, Perth time.

    1. Find a word that fits between the clues. On pitches 1 to 4, type any word that joins them. SUN and FIRE both join LIGHT and STONE. There's no letter count and no single right answer.
    2. Fit and rarity decide your height. Linking every clue earns the whole pitch, up to 1,000 m. Linking one of two earns half. Rarer words climb higher: a common word keeps 60% of its height, a rare one all of it.
    3. Two tries per pitch. You keep the better one. The second try is worth 70%.
    4. Carry the rope. Our link word becomes the first clue of the next pitch.
    5. Name the summit word. Pitch 5 is an uncommon word. Its definition gets clearer after each miss. Three tries: 1,000, 700 or 400 m.
