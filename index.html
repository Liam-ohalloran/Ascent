    How to climb

    One climb a day, the same mountain for everyone. A new one appears at midnight, Perth time.

    1. Begin the climb. You have six minutes for five pitches: The Foothills, The Forest, The Frozen Ridge, The Storm Wall and The Summit. There's one climb a day and no restarts.
    2. Find a word that fits between the clues. SUN and FIRE both join LIGHT and STONE. There's no letter count.
    3. Every real word climbs. Linking every clue earns the most, up to 1,000 m, and rarer fits keep more of it. Linking one clue earns part. A word close to the best one earns 200 m, and any other real word 100 m. Misspelt words are refused without using a try.
    4. Two tries per pitch. The better one counts. The second is worth 70%.
    5. Name the summit word. An uncommon word. Its definition gets clearer after each miss.

    The summit is 4,500 m, so it takes an exceptional climb. Most days end somewhere on the mountain, and that still counts for your streak.
