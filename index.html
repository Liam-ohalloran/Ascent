How to climb

Each day there is one mountain with five pitches. Everyone climbs the same one.

  1. Find a link. On pitches 1 to 4, type any word that joins the clues, like SUN and FIRE both joining LIGHT. There's no letter count.
  2. Every real link earns height. A word that links one of two clues gets half the pitch. Your own word that links them all gets the full 1,000 m.
  3. Carry the rope. Our link word goes up to become the first clue of the next pitch, so the climb is one chain.
  4. Reach the summit. Pitch 5 is an uncommon word. A vague definition gets clearer after each miss, and close guesses are flagged.

You get two tries on each link and keep the better one. The second try is worth 70%. Showing the first letter costs 200 m.

A perfect day is 5,000 m. A new mountain appears at midnight.
